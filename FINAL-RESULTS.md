## How to Create Future Posts (Template)

### Content Structure
```markdown
<p><strong>Quick Summary:</strong> [150-200 words]</p>

<h2>What Is [Topic]?</h2>
<p><strong>Quick Answer:</strong> [40-60 words]</p>
<p>[300-500 words detailed content]</p>

<h2>Why Does [Topic] Matter?</h2>
<p><strong>Quick Answer:</strong> [40-60 words]</p>
<p>[Content with statistics and examples]</p>

[Repeat 5-8 sections]

<table class="data-table">
  <caption>Comparison Title</caption>
  [Table with data + sources in footer]
</table>

<h2>FAQ: Your Questions About [Topic]</h2>
<h3>Question 1?</h3>
<p><strong>Answer:</strong> [100-200 words]</p>
[Repeat 8 times]

<h2>Sources & Further Reading</h2>
[List sources with links]
```

### Add FAQs (Use Rank Math Block)
1. Edit post in WordPress
2. Add block → Search "FAQ" (Rank Math)
3. Add 8 questions with 100-200 word answers
4. Publish → Schema auto-generated!
