HOW TO CREATE HIGH-QUALITY POSTS THAT RANK WELL

Hi! This guide will help you create posts that perform excellently in search engines and provide great value to readers.

================================================================================
QUICK CHECKLIST BEFORE YOU PUBLISH
================================================================================

✓ 2,000+ words - Comprehensive, in-depth content
✓ 8+ FAQ questions answered
✓ 1 table of contents at the beginning
✓ 1 data table with useful information
✓ 5+ headings to organize your content
✓ 3+ internal links to other posts on your site
✓ 1+ external link to authoritative sources
✓ Featured image with descriptive alt text
✓ Meta description written (150-160 characters)

================================================================================
STEP-BY-STEP GUIDE
================================================================================

STEP 1: PLAN YOUR ARTICLE (5 minutes)
--------------------------------------

Before you start writing:
• Choose one main topic - Don't try to cover everything
• Think about reader questions - What would someone want to know?
• Research competitors - What are the top-ranking articles covering?


STEP 2: WRITE AN ENGAGING TITLE (2 minutes)
--------------------------------------------

Good examples:
✓ "Complete Guide to Morocco Travel: 15 Hidden Gems You Can't Miss"
✓ "How to Plan Your First Solo Trip to Southeast Asia (2026 Guide)"
✗ "Travel Tips" (too vague)
✗ "Everything You Need to Know About Everything" (too broad)

PRO TIP: Include numbers, years, or words like "Guide," "How to," or "Complete" in your title.


STEP 3: CREATE YOUR CONTENT STRUCTURE (10 minutes)
---------------------------------------------------

Start by outlining your post with headings:

Main Title (H1)
  • Introduction (H2)
  • Main Topic Section 1 (H2)
    - Subtopic 1.1 (H3)
    - Subtopic 1.2 (H3)
  • Main Topic Section 2 (H2)
    - Subtopic 2.1 (H3)
    - Subtopic 2.2 (H3)
  • Frequently Asked Questions (H2)
  • Conclusion (H2)

Use at least 5-6 headings to break up your content.


STEP 4: WRITE IN-DEPTH CONTENT (60-90 minutes)
-----------------------------------------------

Aim for at least 2,000 words. This might sound like a lot, but if you're truly covering a topic thoroughly, it's actually not that much!

Tips for reaching 2,000 words naturally:
• Explain the "why" behind every point
• Include examples and real stories
• Add personal experiences or case studies
• Describe processes step-by-step
• Include historical context or background
• Address common mistakes or misconceptions

Good paragraph structure:
• Each paragraph: 3-5 sentences
• One idea per paragraph
• Start with a clear topic sentence


STEP 5: ADD A TABLE OF CONTENTS (5 minutes)
--------------------------------------------

Place this near the top of your post, right after your introduction. List all your main headings so readers can jump to what interests them.

Example:

Table of Contents:
1. Why Morocco Should Be Your Next Destination
2. Best Time to Visit Morocco
3. 15 Hidden Gems in Morocco
4. Budget Planning for Your Morocco Trip
5. Frequently Asked Questions
6. Final Thoughts


STEP 6: CREATE A HELPFUL DATA TABLE (10 minutes)
-------------------------------------------------

Add at least one table with useful information. Tables help readers scan information quickly.

Good table examples:
• Budget breakdown by category
• Comparison of destinations/hotels/tours
• Packing list with quantities
• Daily itinerary with times and costs
• Visa requirements by country

Example:

Destination     | Best Season  | Average Cost | Days Needed
----------------|--------------|--------------|-------------
Marrakech       | Oct-April    | $50/day      | 3-4 days
Fes             | Mar-May      | $45/day      | 2-3 days
Chefchaouen     | Apr-Oct      | $40/day      | 2 days


STEP 7: ANSWER FAQS THOROUGHLY (20 minutes)
--------------------------------------------

Include a "Frequently Asked Questions" section with at least 8 questions and answers.

How to find good FAQ questions:
• Check "People Also Ask" box in Google
• Read comments on similar articles
• Think about what confused YOU when you first learned about this topic
• Ask friends or clients what they'd want to know

FAQ formatting example:

Q: How much does a trip to Morocco cost?
A: A budget trip to Morocco costs around $40-60 per day, including accommodation, food, and local transport. Mid-range travelers should budget $80-120 per day, while luxury travelers might spend $200+ per day. This varies by season and cities visited.

PRO TIP: Each answer should be 2-5 sentences - enough to be helpful but not overwhelming.


STEP 8: ADD STRATEGIC LINKS (5 minutes)
----------------------------------------

INTERNAL LINKS (minimum 3):
Link to other relevant posts on YOUR website. This helps readers discover more content and helps Google understand your site structure.

Example: "If you're planning to visit Morocco, check out our complete packing guide for North Africa."

EXTERNAL LINKS (minimum 1):
Link to authoritative sources like:
• Government travel sites
• Research studies
• Official tourism boards
• Respected publications

Example: "According to Morocco's Ministry of Tourism, visitor numbers have increased by..."

Where to place links:
• Naturally within your content (not forced)
• When mentioning statistics or facts
• When referencing related topics
• In your introduction or conclusion


STEP 9: OPTIMIZE YOUR IMAGES (10 minutes)
------------------------------------------

FEATURED IMAGE:
• Size: 1200×630 pixels minimum
• High quality, relevant to your topic
• Compressed for fast loading

ALT TEXT:
Write a brief description for every image (helps SEO and accessibility).

✗ Bad: "image1.jpg"
✓ Good: "Traditional Moroccan riad courtyard with colorful tiles and fountain"

IMAGE QUANTITY:
• Minimum: 1 featured image
• Recommended: 1 image every 300-500 words
• Include: photos, infographics, or custom graphics


STEP 10: WRITE YOUR META DESCRIPTION (3 minutes)
-------------------------------------------------

This is the short text that appears under your title in Google search results.

Requirements:
• 150-160 characters (not too long!)
• Include your main keyword
• Make it compelling - why should someone click?

✗ Bad: "This post is about Morocco travel."
✓ Good: "Discover 15 hidden gems in Morocco that most tourists miss. Complete guide with costs, timing, and insider tips for 2026."


STEP 11: CHOOSE YOUR CATEGORIES AND TAGS (2 minutes)
-----------------------------------------------------

CATEGORIES: 1-2 broad topics (e.g., "Africa Travel," "Budget Travel")
TAGS: 3-6 specific keywords (e.g., "Morocco," "Marrakech," "solo travel," "budget tips")


STEP 12: FINAL REVIEW BEFORE PUBLISHING (10 minutes)
-----------------------------------------------------

Go through this checklist:

CONTENT QUALITY:
□ Is it at least 2,000 words?
□ Does it answer the reader's main question?
□ Are there any spelling or grammar errors?
□ Does each paragraph make sense?

AEO OPTIMIZATION:
□ Table of contents added?
□ At least 8 FAQs included?
□ At least 1 data table added?
□ 5+ headings used?

SEO OPTIMIZATION:
□ Title is clear and includes main keyword?
□ Meta description written (150-160 chars)?
□ Featured image added with alt text?
□ 3+ internal links included?
□ 1+ external authoritative link included?
□ Categories and tags selected?

READABILITY:
□ Short paragraphs (3-5 sentences)?
□ Headings break up the text?
□ No walls of text?
□ Images throughout the post?

================================================================================
COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID
================================================================================

✗ "I'll just write 500 words" - Not enough depth for good rankings
✗ "I don't need FAQs" - FAQs help you rank for question-based searches
✗ "Links don't matter" - Links show authority and help navigation
✗ "One heading is enough" - Readers and Google prefer well-structured content
✗ "Alt text is optional" - It helps SEO and accessibility
✗ "I'll skip the table of contents" - TOCs improve user experience significantly

================================================================================
TIME INVESTMENT
================================================================================

First few posts: 2-3 hours each
After you get the hang of it: 90 minutes - 2 hours
Worth it? Absolutely! Quality posts rank higher and bring more readers.
